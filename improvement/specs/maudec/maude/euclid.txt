Write a Maude functional module computing the greatest common divisor of two
natural numbers with the Euclidean algorithm.

Required interface (terms of this exact form must parse and reduce):
- an operator named $gcd (with the leading dollar sign) taking two Nat
  arguments and returning a Nat, based on the built-in NAT module.

Behaviour:
- $gcd(m, 0) = m.
- otherwise $gcd(m, n) is the gcd of n and the remainder of m divided by n.

Examples: $gcd(12, 8) reduces to 4; $gcd(0, 5) reduces to 5.
