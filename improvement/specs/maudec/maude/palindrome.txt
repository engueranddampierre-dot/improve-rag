Write a Maude functional module deciding whether a list of vowels is a
palindrome.

Required interface (terms of this exact form must parse and reduce):
- sorts Symbol and List with Symbol a subsort of List;
- five constant constructors a e i o u of sort Symbol;
- a constant nil of sort List (the empty list);
- list concatenation written by JUXTAPOSITION (an operator __ ), declared
  associative with identity nil, so that a e a is a valid List term;
- palindrome : List -> Bool.

Behaviour:
- palindrome(nil) and palindrome of a single symbol are true;
- a list reading the same forwards and backwards is a palindrome;
- anything else reduces to false.

Examples: palindrome(a e a) reduces to true; palindrome(a e) to false.
