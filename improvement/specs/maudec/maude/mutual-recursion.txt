Write a Maude functional module with two mutually recursive functions on
natural numbers.

Required interface (terms of this exact form must parse and reduce):
- two operators f and g, each taking one Nat and returning a Nat, based on
  the built-in NAT module.

Behaviour:
- f(0) = 1.
- f(n + 1) = 2 + g(n).
- g(n) = 1 + f(n) for every n.

Examples: f(0) reduces to 1; g(0) reduces to 2; f(1) reduces to 4.
