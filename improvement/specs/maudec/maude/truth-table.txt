Write a Maude functional module defining logical conjunction by explicit
truth tables (built-in BOOL module).

Required interface (terms of this exact form must parse and reduce):
- an operator and taking two Bool arguments and returning a Bool, defined
  by its COMPLETE truth table: one equation per combination of true/false
  (four equations);
- an operator andc taking two Bool arguments and returning a Bool, declared
  COMMUTATIVE (the comm attribute), defined with the MINIMAL number of
  equations that commutativity allows (the mixed case needs only one).

Behaviour: both operators compute standard logical conjunction.

Examples: and(true, false) reduces to false; andc(false, true) to false;
andc(true, true) to true.
