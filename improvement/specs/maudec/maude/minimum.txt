Write a Maude functional module computing the minimum of two natural numbers.

Required interface (terms of this exact form must parse and reduce):
- an operator minimum taking two Nat arguments and returning a Nat, declared
  COMMUTATIVE (the comm attribute), based on the built-in NAT module.

Behaviour:
- minimum(m, n) reduces to the smaller of the two numbers.
- Commutativity must be declared as an equational attribute of the operator,
  not encoded through extra equations.

Examples: minimum(3, 7) reduces to 3; minimum(7, 3) reduces to 3.
