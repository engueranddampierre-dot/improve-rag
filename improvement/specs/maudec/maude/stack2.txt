Write a Maude PARAMETERIZED functional module implementing stacks over an
arbitrary element theory, plus an instantiation to natural numbers extended
with three stack-transforming operations.

Required interface, part 1 — parameterized stack (as in a classic STACK
module over the trivial theory TRIV, parameter X):
- sort Stack{X}; constructors mt (empty stack) and push(element, stack);
- pop (pop of empty is empty), top (partial, last pushed element),
  empty? (true exactly on mt).

Required interface, part 2 — a module instantiating the stack with Nat and
adding (terms of this exact form must parse and reduce):
- topIncr : Stack{Nat} -> Stack{Nat}, incrementing only the top element
  (empty stack unchanged);
- incr : Stack{Nat} -> Stack{Nat}, incrementing every element;
- merge : Stack{Nat} -> Stack{Nat}, replacing the top TWO elements by their
  sum; a stack with fewer than two elements is returned unchanged.

Examples: topIncr(push(1, push(2, mt))) reduces to push(2, push(2, mt));
incr(push(1, push(2, mt))) to push(2, push(3, mt));
merge(push(1, push(2, mt))) to push(3, mt).
