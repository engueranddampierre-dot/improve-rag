Write a Maude functional module testing whether a natural number is even,
in two independent ways.

Required interface (terms of this exact form must parse and reduce):
- two operators even1 and even2, each taking one Nat and returning a Bool,
  based on the built-in NAT and BOOL modules.

Behaviour:
- even1(n) and even2(n) both reduce to true when n is even, false when odd.
- even1 must decide using arithmetic on n (e.g. divisibility by 2).
- even2 must decide by structural recursion on n, descending two units at a
  time with base cases for 0 and 1.

Examples: even1(4) and even2(4) reduce to true; even1(7) and even2(7) to false.
