Write a Maude functional module illustrating subsorting and sort-membership
tests.

Required interface (terms of this exact form must parse and reduce):
- sorts Foo and Bar with Foo a subsort of Bar;
- constant constructors f of sort Foo and g of sort Bar;
- isFoo1 : Bar -> Bool, deciding with Maude's built-in sort-membership test
  whether its argument belongs to sort Foo;
- isFoo2 : Bar -> Bool, deciding the same property WITHOUT the membership
  test, by pattern-matching on the argument sort (with a default case);
- isPositive : Int -> Bool, true exactly on strictly positive integers,
  decided by matching on the argument's sort within the built-in INT sort
  hierarchy (non-zero naturals versus the rest), not by an arithmetic
  comparison.

Examples: isFoo1(f) and isFoo2(f) reduce to true; isFoo1(g) and isFoo2(g)
to false; isPositive(3) to true; isPositive(-3) and isPositive(0) to false.
