Write a Maude functional module implementing the EXTENDED Euclidean
algorithm on integers.

Required interface (terms of this exact form must parse and reduce):
- a sort EuclidResult with a free constructor euc taking three Int
  arguments (built-in INT module);
- an operator eeuclid taking two Int arguments and returning a EuclidResult;
- you may add auxiliary operators if needed.

Behaviour:
- eeuclid(m, n) reduces to euc(d, u, v) where d is the greatest common
  divisor of m and n, and u, v are Bezout coefficients: u * m + v * n = d.
- The coefficients must match the classical iterative extended Euclidean
  algorithm that starts from (m, 1, 0) and (n, 0, 1) and updates both pairs
  with the successive integer quotients.

Example: eeuclid(12, 8) reduces to euc(4, 1, -1).
