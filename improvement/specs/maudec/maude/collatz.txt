Write a Maude SYSTEM module (with rewrite rules, not equations) modelling one
step of the Collatz process on natural numbers.

Required interface:
- no new operators: the states are the built-in Nat numbers themselves, and
  the dynamics are given by conditional rewrite rules on a Nat variable.

Behaviour (one-step rewriting):
- an even number n may rewrite to n divided by 2 (integer division);
- an odd number n may rewrite to 3 * n + 1.

Example: the term 6 rewrites in one step to 3; the term 7 rewrites in one
step to 22.
