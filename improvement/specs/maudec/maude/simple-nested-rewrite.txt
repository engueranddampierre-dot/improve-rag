Write a Maude SYSTEM module (with rewrite rules) illustrating nested
rewriting and the frozen attribute.

Required interface (terms of this exact form must parse):
- sorts Foo, Bar and Ter;
- a constructor f taking (Bar, Foo) and returning Foo;
- three constant constructors a b c of sort Bar;
- a constructor g taking one Bar and returning Ter, declared FROZEN on its
  first (and only) argument, so that no rewriting happens under g;
- two labelled rewrite rules: one rewriting the constant a to b, one
  rewriting b to c.

Behaviour (one-step rewriting):
- a rewrites to b, and b rewrites to c, anywhere in a term EXCEPT under the
  frozen operator g: the term g(a) admits no rewrite at all.
